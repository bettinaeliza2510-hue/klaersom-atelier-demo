The process

Three steps, one pattern

No login, no software to install. Everything below runs right here in your browser.

01 · Measure

Take eight simple body measurements, or start from an illustrative sample size and adjust from there.

02 · Design

Pick a neckline and skirt silhouette, then dial in fullness and sleeve puff — or let Gemini read a reference image.

03 · Draft

Get dot-to-dot pattern pieces with cut counts, fabric estimate and seam checks — ready to scale onto paper.
