How the drafting formulas work
  • Bodice width = bust ÷ 4 + 1 cm per half piece (4 cm total ease). Waist = waist ÷ 4 + 0.5 cm (2 cm total ease).
  • Armhole depth = bust ÷ 6 + 7 cm. The armhole and round necklines are quadratic Bezier curves sampled into dots.
  • Shoulder tip = half the shoulder width; the shoulder slopes down 2.5 cm.
  • Skirt top = bodice waist width × fullness; hem = at least quarter hip + 1 cm.
  • Sleeve width = (upper arm + 4 cm) × puff; cap height = one third of armhole depth.
  • Seam allowance shifts every edge outwards and re-joins the neighbours. Fold edges get none; the skirt hem gets a larger one.
